**Problem 4.15 (ANMs)**

1.  Consider the SCM

$$
\begin{aligned}
      X &:= N_X \\
      Y &:= 2X + N_Y
      \end{aligned}
$$

with $N_X$ uniformly distributed between $1$ and $3$ and $N_Y$ uniformly distributed between $-0.5$ and $0.5$ and independent of $N_X$. The distribution $P_{X,Y}$ admits an ANM from $X$ to $Y$. Draw the support of the joint distribution of $X,Y$ and convince yourself that $P_{X,Y}$ does not admit an ANM from $Y$ to $X$, that is, there is no function $g$ and independent noise variables $M_X$ and $M_Y$ such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
      X &= g(Y) + M_X \\
      Y &= M_Y
      \end{aligned}
$$

with $M_X$ independent of $M_Y$.

**Solution** Clearly $P\{X < 1 \lor X > 3 \} = 0.$ Also clearly $\operatorname{supp} Y = [1.5, 6.5]$

$$
p(x) = \begin{cases}
 1/2, \text{ if } x \in [1,3] \\
 0, \text{ if } x \notin [1,3]
\end{cases}
$$

$$
p(n) = \begin{cases}
 1, \text{ if } x \in [-1/2, 1/2] \\
 0, \text{ if } x \notin [-1/2,1/2]
\end{cases}
$$

Recall convolution $p(y) = p_n(n) + \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} p_x(\frac{y-n}{2}) dn$ or $p(y) = \int p_x(x) p_n(y-2x) dx$

So we get

$$
p(y \mid x) = p_n(y-2x) = \begin{cases}
 1, & \text{if } y-2x \in [-1/2, 1/2] \\
 0, & \text{if } y-2x \notin [-1/2, 1/2]
\end{cases}
$$

Multiplying the marginal by the conditional gives the joint density

$$
p(x,y) = p(x)\,p(y \mid x) = \begin{cases}
 1/2, & \text{if } x \in [1,3] \text{ and } |y - 2x| \le 1/2 \\
 0,   & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

so the support is the slanted band

$$
\operatorname{supp} P_{X,Y} = \left\{ (x,y) : 1 \le x \le 3,\ 2x - \tfrac12 \le y \le 2x + \tfrac12 \right\},
$$

a parallelogram with vertices $(1,\tfrac32), (1,\tfrac52), (3,\tfrac{11}{2}), (3,\tfrac{13}{2})$.

Now read the band the other way. Conditioning on $Y = y$,

$$
X \mid Y = y \ \sim\ \mathcal{U}\left[ \max\left(1, \tfrac{y}{2} - \tfrac14\right),\ \min\left(3, \tfrac{y}{2} + \tfrac14\right) \right].
$$

For $y \in [\tfrac52, \tfrac{11}{2}]$ neither bound binds and the interval is $\left[\tfrac{y}{2} - \tfrac14, \tfrac{y}{2} + \tfrac14\right]$: width $\tfrac12$, a pure translate of a fixed set, which is exactly what an ANM needs. But for $y \in [\tfrac32, \tfrac52)$ the constraint $x \ge 1$ truncates it to $[1, \tfrac{y}{2} + \tfrac14]$, of width $\tfrac{y}{2} - \tfrac34 < \tfrac12$, and symmetrically near $y = \tfrac{13}{2}$.

**Why this rules out an ANM (Definition 4.4, p. 67).** An ANM from $Y$ to $X$ means $X = g(Y) + M_X$ with $M_X \perp\!\!\!\perp Y$. Independence gives, for bounded measurable $h$ and Borel $B$, by Fubini,

$$
\mathbb{E}[h(X)\mathbf{1}_B(Y)] = \int_B \mathbb{E}[h(g(y)+M_X)]\,P_Y(dy),
$$

so for $P_Y$-a.e. $y$ the conditional law $P_{X\mid Y=y}$ is the law of $g(y)+M_X$. Hence $\operatorname{Var}(X \mid Y=y) = \operatorname{Var}(M_X)$ is a.e. constant in $y$. But from the conditional above, $\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=y) = \tfrac{1}{48}$ for $y \in (\tfrac52,\tfrac{11}{2})$ and $= \tfrac{1}{12}(\tfrac{y}{2}-\tfrac34)^2 < \tfrac1{48}$ for $y \in (\tfrac32,\tfrac52)$, and both sets have positive $P_Y$-mass. Contradiction: there is no ANM from $Y$ to $X$. (Theorem 4.5, p. 67–68, does not apply: it requires strictly positive, smooth densities.)
